I immediately said I was not interested because I get these types of calls almost weekly. The caller was persistent and a good talker. He got me to take my email address and send me an email. He later asked for my credit card, and I said no. I told him this was a scam. I hung up.

He called again and asked me to talk to his supervisor. He said my credit card was not required but asked me to share my passport number for verification purposes. I said no.

I left out a lot of details to keep it short. But the scammer was good.

Good communication skills, negotiation skills, and persistence are essential to success, along with hard work.

- Good communication comes from preparing the content and delivery before taking action.
- Good negotiation comes from examples that have had an impact.
- Persistence comes from the idea that the approach has to be continuous and broad.

I will have to apply my own advice in practice tomorrow.
