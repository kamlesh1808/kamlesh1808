A Software Engineer is the central and driving role in IT product engineering.

In building a software product feature, a software engineer works and collaborates with a product owner, business analysts, QA analysts, data engineers, DBAs, system administrators, cloud engineers, project managers, business stakeholders, and a team of software engineers every single day.

Over time, new roles are created, such as AI and ML Engineer. Some roles transform, for example, a business analyst into a product manager or a system administrator into a cloud engineer.

In all this, one role stays mostly constant: a software engineer who writes code to develop a product feature that adds value to business users.

Yes, more and more software engineers use AI assistants and agents to code and refactor faster.

A software engineer continuously evolves to work with new IT roles, learns emerging technologies, and adapts to new or remodelled processes in the industry and their organization.

It is a very challenging but rewarding role if you are into building.

A builder Software Engineer is always in demand.
