When software engineers build large business functions, the inputs, processes, and data outputs are generally defined.

Subsequently, doing data cleansing and data relation mapping across multiple business domain microservices is fine. However, I think data architecture and design should be embedded early into the software engineering of the business function.

This means business data is relatable across the organization. This data is then much easier to consume.
