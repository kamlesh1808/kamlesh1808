Software engineering is more than coding.

- It is planning, communicating, and executing.
- It is analyzing customers' needs and building and delivering a solution in a timely and well-communicated manner.
- It is managing business stakeholders' expectations.
- It is designing a solution that meets both technical and business requirements.
- It is unit testing, functional testing, and performance testing.
- It is documenting business and technical details from the start of production implementation through the end of the product lifecycle.
- It is collaborating with the team, including developers, QA testers, business analysts, DevOps engineers, product owners, and business stakeholders.
- It is iterative.
